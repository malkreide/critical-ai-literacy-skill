A Claude Skill for critical-theoretical analysis of AI systems, digital platforms and technology decisions. Based on Critical Theory (Frankfurt School), Critical Data Studies and AI Ethics — calibrated for Swiss public administration and education.

> *"No algorithmic system is neutral. Every AI application encodes decisions about what is optimized, whose data counts and which worldview is considered 'objective.'"*

## Overview

This skill enables structured, theoretically grounded analysis of AI systems, focusing on:

- **Power Structures** — Who gains, who loses?
- **Algorithmic Bias** — What is built in, whom does it disadvantage?
- **Data Politics** — Whose data, whose rules?
- **Societal Impacts** — What does this mean for democracy, education and public services?

## The 6-Step Analysis Framework

| Step | Focus | Guiding Question |
|------|-------|------------------|
| 1. System Mapping | Technical & institutional context | *What are we looking at?* |
| 2. Power Analysis | Knowledge, decision-making power, economy, representation | *Who benefits, who loses?* |
| 3. Bias Analysis | Systematic distortions & discrimination | *What biases are built in?* |
| 4. Narrative Analysis | Legitimation narratives & validity claims | *What story is being told?* |
| 5. Perspective Shift | Missing voices, resonance, one-dimensionality | *Who was not heard?* |
| 6. Design Recommendations | Governance, technology, organization | *How can it be done better?* |

Each analysis closes with an **overall assessment** (🟢🟡🔴🚫) and a **red line check**.

## Theoretical Foundation

The skill draws from a rich tradition of critical thinking, translated for the digital age:

| Thinker | Classical Concept | Digital Application |
|---------|-------------------|---------------------|
| Horkheimer/Adorno | Instrumental Reason | Algorithmic Rationality |
| Habermas | Communicative Reason | Standard for AI Communication |
| Honneth | Recognition/Disrespect | Algorithmic (Non-)Recognition |
| Marcuse | One-Dimensional Man | One-Dimensional Optimization |
| Rosa | Resonance/Alienation | Algorithmic Alienation |
| Freire | Banking Concept | LLMs as Ultimate Knowledge Banks |

Complemented by contemporary frameworks: Algorithmic Bias (Buolamwini, Noble), Data Extractivism (Couldry/Mejias), Opacity (Pasquale), Stochastic Parrots (Bender et al.).

## Quick Modes

Not every inquiry requires the full 6-step analysis:

- **Quick Check** (5 min.) — Five core questions for an initial assessment
- **Narrative Check** (10 min.) — Critical review of strategy papers and press releases
- **Procurement Review** (30 min.) — Systematic review before procurement decisions

## Swiss Calibration 🇨🇭

This skill is specifically calibrated for:

- Swiss data protection legislation (revDSG, cantonal IDG)
- EU AI Act as reference framework (risk categories)
- Transparency and equal treatment obligations in public procurement
- Democratic legitimacy of technology decisions
- Swiss multilingualism as a touchstone for AI systems
- Digital sovereignty and dependency on international tech corporations

## Data Quality Indicators

Every assessment is marked with its evidence base:

- 🔵 **Substantiated** — Based on verifiable facts, audits, published studies
- ⚪ **Estimate** — Based on assumptions, analogies or incomplete information

This distinction is critical for executive decisions: Recommendations based on ⚪ estimates require validation.
